Refund Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

We want you to be happy with Tweakly Pro. If it isn't right for you, you can get a full refund.

14-day money-back guarantee

Ask within 14 days of your purchase and we'll refund you in full. No questions asked.

How to ask for a refund

  • Email us with your order number or the email address you used to buy, or
  • Use the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email.

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

After a refund

The refunded license key is disabled, and Tweakly returns to the free plan (three tweaks at a time). You can keep using Tweakly for free.

After 14 days

We're not obliged to refund purchases older than 14 days, but if something is wrong, email us and we'll do our best to help.

This policy doesn't affect your statutory rights.
