Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Tweakly is made by Sajid Shaik ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Tweakly app and this website. The short version: Tweakly doesn't collect personal data.

The app

Tweakly runs entirely on your Mac. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

  • Accessibility permission. Tweakly uses it to react to a few keys (such as Return, ⌘X and Esc) in specific apps, and to move or close windows. Keystrokes are handled instantly on your Mac and are never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Settings. Which tweaks you turn on, and a few preferences, are stored locally on your Mac.
  • Notifications. Shown locally by macOS. Nothing is sent to us.

Licenses and payments

Purchases are handled by our reseller and merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy, which processes your payment and email address under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.

When you activate Tweakly Pro, the app sends your license key and your Mac's name to Lemon Squeezy so the license can be checked and you can manage which Macs use it. While Pro is active, the app quietly re-checks the license about once a month. Nothing else is sent.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

You can remove Tweakly at any time by quitting it and moving it to the Trash. To ask about or delete purchase records, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
