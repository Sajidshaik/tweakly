FAQ

Good questions.

Why does Tweakly ask for Accessibility permission?

To notice shortcuts like Return or ⌘X in Finder and to move or close windows. It's the same permission window managers and keyboard tools use. Tweakly only reacts to the few keys its tweaks need, and only in the apps they're for.

Does Tweakly collect any data?

No. There are no analytics or accounts. The only time Tweakly goes online is to activate or occasionally re-check a Pro license with our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy. See the privacy policy.

Will it slow my Mac down?

No. Tweakly sleeps until a tweak needs it, and only the tweaks you switch on run at all.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

Apple doesn't allow App Store apps to use the Accessibility features Tweakly needs. Tweakly is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS knows it's safe to open.

How many Macs can I use Pro on?

Two of your own Macs. Moving to a new one? Deactivate the old Mac from Tweakly's menu and activate the new one.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — within 14 days, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

Something's misbehaving. How do I turn it off?

Flip that tweak's switch, or pause everything with the switch at the top of Tweakly's menu. macOS goes straight back to normal. Still stuck? Email us.
